Overview

What you are actually buying

74.3 m² internal 3 Bedrooms · 2 Bathrooms · Store 1 unit / 40HQ 30 days lead time

The 40FT Expandable is the largest single unit we build: 11800 mm long, expanding to 11800 × 6300 mm for 74.3 m² of internal floor. That is comparable to a small conventional house, and it is the point at which buyers stop making compromises on room sizes.

The standard layout gives three bedrooms, two full bathrooms, a kitchen with room for an island, a living/dining area that takes a six-seat table, and a walk-in store that most people convert into a laundry or pantry. Because the total footprint is generous, the layout can be pushed further than the smaller models: four bedrooms, a second living area, or a fully open-plan living/kitchen with a separate utility room.

Structurally it shares the same system as the rest of the range — galvanised bolted frame, factory-hinged wings, sandwich-panel envelope — but with the longest span in the catalogue, which makes it the model most often ordered for two-storey stacking on project sites.

It ships one unit per 40HQ. For volume buyers that matters: at 50 units the freight per unit is roughly 30% higher than the 30ft, and we will show you both numbers side by side so the decision is based on landed cost rather than FOB.

Installation

Six steps, two people, no wet trades

Every unit ships with a written installation manual, a marked-up bolt drawing and the torque figures. We also run a video call with your installer before the container arrives — that call has prevented more problems than any document.

Toolkit required: socket set, torque wrench, silicone gun, spirit or laser level, a forklift or small crane. No welding equipment.

Get the installation manual

Build the base for the load

A 74.3 m² unit with water and furniture can exceed 8,000 kg. Use a slab, or support beams at 3000 mm centres with proper footings. We supply a base drawing with the point loads marked.

Plan the lift

At 11800 mm folded the unit needs a crane or a large telehandler with a spreader beam, not a forklift. Most buyers arrange this through a local crane hire firm — we provide the rigging drawing.

Land and level

Set the unit on its base and level it across both axes with the adjustable feet before releasing the lifting gear. Do not skip this: an out-of-level base shows up as door alignment problems three weeks later.

Unfold the wings

Release the transport locks and extend both wings — about 25 minutes per wing with two people. The four-panel design on this model means each wing panel is shorter and easier to handle than on the 20ft.

Bolt the structure

Torque the wing-to-frame and wing-to-wing connections to the figures on the supplied drawing, then check the three diagonal measurements.

Commission services

Two wet areas, so allow an extra hour for the water test. Confirm the RCD trips, the hot water runs, and the roof drainage discharges away from the base.
