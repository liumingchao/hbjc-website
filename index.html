Why expandable, not traditional

Six reasons buyers stop building on site

An expandable house is a finished house folded into a steel frame. Everything that normally eats your schedule and budget — tiling, plumbing, wiring, painting — happens in a factory on a jig, not in the rain on your land.

30-minute setup, 2 people

Ship it folded, bolt the four corners, unfold the wings, connect the pre-wired plugs. A forklift or a small crane is enough — no concrete foundation required, levelled blocks or a steel skid will do.

1/5 the shipping volume

Folded, a 20ft unit is only 2.26 m wide, so two units fit one 40HQ. That is the single biggest line item in your landed cost — and it is where expandable beats a fixed container home by 40–60%.

Galvanised steel, 25-year shell

Q235B/Q355B galvanised frame, 0.5 mm colour-steel cladding over 75 mm EPS / rock-wool / PU sandwich panel. Load tested to 1.5 kN/m² floor and 1.0 kN/m² roof — the usual 50-year design wind and snow codes.

Built to your code, not ours

We build to your spec: AU/NZS, US (IBC/IRC), EU (EN 1090), or Middle-East wind loads. 110 V / 220 V wiring, R-value insulation and door sizes are set before production starts.

Move it, resell it, restack it

It stays a container. Relocate a site camp, split a building into rental units, stack two floors for a mining or oilfield camp — with no demolition cost and no loss of the shell.

25–35 day production

Standard models ship in 25 days, custom layout in 30–35. We keep 400–500 units moving out every month, so your order is not waiting behind someone else's.

How an order runs

From first message to keys in hand

  1. STEP 01

    Inquiry & config

    Send model, quantity and destination port. We reply with FOB and CIF options and a drawing, usually within 12 hours.

  2. STEP 02

    Drawing approval

    We issue a 2D layout and 3D preview for your sign-off. Nothing is cut until you approve it in writing.

  3. STEP 03

    30% deposit, production

    Production starts on deposit. Weekly photo updates in a shared folder — you always know which stage your units are at.

  4. STEP 04

    Inspection & loading

    Third-party inspection welcome (SGS/BV). We load, photograph every unit and send you the loading report and container numbers.

  5. STEP 05

    On-site support

    Video guidance during unfolding, plus a written 12-page installation manual and spare-parts list in every container.
