Overview

What you are actually buying

56.7 m² internal 3 Bedrooms · 1 Bathroom 1–2 units / 40HQ 25–30 days lead time

The 30FT Expandable is 9000 mm long when folded and 9000 × 6300 mm when opened, giving 56.7 m² of internal floor. That puts it in the sweet spot for secondary dwellings: big enough for three bedrooms and a proper living area, small enough to stay inside the floor-area caps that most councils apply to granny flats and ADUs.

Structurally it is the same system as the 20ft — galvanised bolted frame, factory-hinged wings, sandwich-panel walls — but with the extra length working for you. The 9000 mm span means the living/dining room is column-free, so you can put a full dining table and a sofa group in without walking around a post.

Standard layout gives three bedrooms, one bathroom, a kitchen and a combined living/dining area. The two layouts we quote most often are 2 bedrooms plus a study (for people who work from home) and 2 bedrooms plus a second bathroom (for shared occupancy). Neither costs more than the standard drawing.

Because floor area drives approval in most markets, this is the model where our compliance documentation does the most work. For Australia we supply the AS/NZS accessory compliance pack; for the US, the drawings and material declarations that an IBC/IRC reviewer will ask for; for Europe, EN 1090 structural certification.

Installation

Six steps, two people, no wet trades

Every unit ships with a written installation manual, a marked-up bolt drawing and the torque figures. We also run a video call with your installer before the container arrives — that call has prevented more problems than any document.

Toolkit required: socket set, torque wrench, silicone gun, spirit or laser level, a forklift or small crane. No welding equipment.

Get the installation manual

Prepare a level base

Two rows of support at 3000 mm centres minimum. Slab, compacted hardcore, or blocks. Flatness within 10 mm across the 9 m length — for a base this long, check it with a laser level, not by eye.

Land the folded unit

Forklift or small crane lifts at the four marked lugs. At 9 m folded the unit is long but narrow (2260 mm), so a standard flatbed can move it from port to site.

Unfold both wings

Release the transport locks and swing each wing out until it seats on the frame rail. Allow about 20 minutes per wing with two people. The wing panels are the heaviest part of the whole job.

Bolt and align

Torque the wing-to-frame and wing-to-wing bolts in the sequence shown on the supplied drawing. Check the diagonal measurements at this point — the drawing gives you the figures.

Connect services

One power feed, one water inlet, one drain. Internal circuits and the wet-area pipework are already terminated in the factory.

Seal and commission

Silicone the external joints, water-test the bathroom, test the RCD, and check that all bedroom doors latch after the base has settled for 24 hours.
