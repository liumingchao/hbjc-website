The factory behind the container

杰诚 Modular House Co., Ltd. has manufactured modular buildings in Hebei since 2010. We own the workshop, the production lines and the drawings — which is why we can tell you a delivery date and keep it, and why you are welcome to send an inspector before you pay.

Who we are

A manufacturer that sells direct — nothing in between

We started in 2010 building flat pack container houses for construction sites in northern China. In 2017 we designed our first expandable unit because customers kept asking for something that unfolded into a habitable house instead of a room. That product now accounts for most of what leaves the gate.

There is no trading company between you and the workshop. The person who quotes your order is the person who can walk onto the floor and check it. When you ask why a delivery slipped, you get the actual reason — a steel price move, a panel line stoppage, a port strike — not a padded explanation from a middleman.

We ship to 60+ countries, mostly to buyers who order again. Repeat orders make up the majority of our volume, and the reason is simple: the drawing you approve is the drawing we build from.

Certification

Paperwork that clears your customs and your building inspector

Certification is where container house deals quietly fall apart. We prepare the documents before the container leaves, not after your broker asks for them.

Request the certificate pack

CE marking

EU Declaration of Performance for the structural and envelope systems, with the test reports behind it.

ISO 9001:2015

Factory quality management system, audited annually. Covers traceability from steel coil to finished unit.

SGS / BV factory audit

Third-party factory capability reports, plus pre-shipment inspection on request.

EN 1090 steel structure

Execution class certification for the welded frame components and the drawing control that goes with it.

AS/NZS compliance pack

Australian and New Zealand market: compliant electrical accessories, glazing and structural documentation.

UL-listed components

For the US and Canada: 110 V electrical components and fittings carrying UL listings accepted by local inspectors.

Milestones

Sixteen years, five moments that mattered

2010

Founded in Zhengding, Hebei, making flat pack container houses for construction sites.

2013

First export order. Learned the hard way that documentation matters as much as the product.

2017

Designed and patented the factory-hinged expandable frame. The product that defines the company.

2020

Workshop expanded past 50,000 m²; third automatic panel line installed.

2023

Apple cabin line added. Annual output passes 5,000 units across all product families.
