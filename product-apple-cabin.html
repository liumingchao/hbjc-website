Overview

What you are actually buying

12.8 m² internal Studio · 1 Bathroom 4 units / 40HQ 35 days lead time

The Apple Cabin is not an expandable house — it is a single-piece capsule, 5800 × 2200 × 2500 mm, delivered fully assembled and finished. There is nothing to unfold and no site assembly beyond setting it down on a prepared base and connecting power and water.

What defines it is the glazing: one entire long wall is tempered double glazing in an aluminium frame, running from floor to ceiling. That single design decision is why resorts, glamping sites and holiday parks buy it, and why it earns more per night than a container conversion of the same footprint.

Inside, the standard fit-out is a studio: sleeping zone, compact kitchenette, and a full bathroom with shower, basin and WC. Finishes are a step above our expandable range — SPC flooring, aluminium trim, concealed LED lighting, and a choice of interior panelling including a curved option that follows the capsule shell.

Four units fit one 40HQ container, so the freight per unit on a multi-unit order is the lowest in our entire range. For a resort adding six pods, that is a materially different business case than importing six separate structures.

Installation

Six steps, two people, no wet trades

Every unit ships with a written installation manual, a marked-up bolt drawing and the torque figures. We also run a video call with your installer before the container arrives — that call has prevented more problems than any document.

Toolkit required: socket set, torque wrench, silicone gun, spirit or laser level, a forklift or small crane. No welding equipment.

Get the installation manual

Prepare a small, flat base

The unit weighs about 1,800 kg, so four pads or a compacted gravel pad is enough. Flatness within 5 mm — the shell is rigid, and an uneven base is visible through the glazing line.

Plan access for the delivery truck

Fully assembled, the cabin is 5800 mm long and 2500 mm high. Check overhead cables, gate widths and turning space before the truck arrives — this step causes more delays than any other.

Set it down

A truck-mounted crane, a small crawler crane or a telehandler. Four marked lifting points, one smooth lift, set on the prepared pads.

Level and fix

Level with the built-in adjustable feet, then anchor to the pads with the supplied brackets if your site is exposed to high wind.

Connect power and water

One power feed, one water inlet and one drain. All internal circuits, plumbing and the extractor fan are factory-terminated.

Commission and dress

Test the RCD, run the shower, check the glazing seals, and fit the supplied interior curtains or blinds. A single pod is a half-day job for two people.
